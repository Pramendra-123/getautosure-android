GetAutoSure box, insert, card and sticker

A premium-looking retail box for the GetAutoSure OBD-II scanner. It is built on the vendor's own spec (100 × 63 × 25.5 mm, 350 GSM, matte lamination, orange die-cut insert), so the main change is artwork, not cost.

Selling price ₹999 on Amazon · suggested MRP ₹1,499 · one box for all buyers (no separate Dealer Sathi box) · drawings are in millimetres

The card and sticker can be printed together on one sheet, which keeps setup cost to a single job.

The box · option B (recommended)

Flip-lid opening, same size and board

The lid opens on the long edge like the Carly reference. The quick-start steps and QR code are printed inside the lid, so the buyer sees them first and no separate manual is needed. The vendor only needs a new cutting die. Board, size, lamination and insert stay the same.

The box · option A (fallback)

Keep the vendor's tuck-end carton, change the artwork

If the vendor won't make a new die, keep their current upright tuck-end box and only replace the print. Drop the window, because the dongle is ugly through plastic and removing it saves a step. Use the same black, orange band and mark. The quick-start steps and QR code move to the back panel.

CE and FCC logos

The current box prints CE and FC marks. Only print them if the vendor gives you the test reports for this exact model. If they can't, leave the marks off. The same applies to any Bluetooth (WPC) approval Amazon may ask for.

The dongle

One clean face, matching the homepage video

Today's shell has five separate prints. The target is the face from the website video: wordmark, LED window, app icon and two lines of text. Ask the vendor to apply it as one printed label in the recessed front panel. That is the easiest change for them, and the label can be printed in the same job as the card and sticker.

Remove

  • "OBD Advanced" heading
  • Yellow engine icon and "CHECK Fault Codes"
  • Gauge drawing and "Realtime Information"
  • Green "Bluetooth Scan tool" line
  • "VN:" serial on the front (move it to the back or underside)

Keep or add

  • Wordmark: "GetAuto" in white, "Sure" in orange
  • LED window with Power / PC / OBD labels
  • Orange app icon, centred
  • "OBD-II Car Scanner" and "Bluetooth · getautosure.com"
  • Matte black shell, no gloss
The card · 95 × 58 mm

Upgrade offer on the front, port finder on the back

People throw away cards that only sell something. The useful back side means this one stays in the glovebox, and the upgrade offer stays with it.

The sticker · Ø 50 mm

"Checked by GetAutoSure"

One sticker goes in every box. Owners put it on their own car. Dealers stick it on cars they have scanned, and buyers on the lot see your brand for free.

  • Material: clear static cling, so it sticks to the inside of the glass with no glue and can be moved from car to car. Ask the vendor to print it in reverse for the inside of the glass.
  • Cheaper fallback: a plain vinyl sticker for the outside of the glass.
  • Dealers: sell extra stickers in packs of 20 later. They don't need their own box.
Decisions

Price, QR codes and open questions

MRP: ₹1,499

At ₹999 this shows as 33% off, which looks believable. ₹1,999 shows as 50% off, which buyers often read as an inflated MRP on a ₹999 product. The MRP is printed on the box, so decide before the print run.

QR codes that never change

Print getautosure.com/app and getautosure.com/pro. Until the app is live, /app shows a coming-soon page. After launch, point it at the Play Store link. The printed boxes never need to change.

Print QR codes dark on a white square, at least 15 mm wide, and test-scan the printer's proof.

Ask the vendor

  1. Exact dongle size (L × W × H). At 25.5 mm the box is tight, so the insert needs a full-depth cut-out, not a raised tray.
  2. One-time cost for a flip-lid cutting die (option B) vs. reusing their tuck-end die (option A).
  3. Extra cost per box for printing inside the lid.
  4. Can they apply a front label to the dongle instead of their current prints?
  5. Price for the card and the static-cling sticker at your order quantity, and the MOQ.
  6. Test reports for CE, FCC and Bluetooth (WPC) for this model, or confirmation to leave the marks off.
  7. A printed physical proof of the orange before the full run. Aim close to Pantone 1585 C.
