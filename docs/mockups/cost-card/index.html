Rules behind the card

  • Shows only when something changed: mileage down 8%+ against the car's own usual, a long-idling drive, or a fault that appeared mid-drive. Otherwise no card.
  • One card at a time, the costliest first. Dismissed cards don't come back until something new happens.
  • ₹ only where measured: the mileage-drop cost and idling (fuel rate from the car, or a standard idle rate when the car doesn't report it). No invented ₹ for driving style.
  • Fuel price: by registration state, refreshed monthly; ₹103 petrol, ₹90 diesel, ₹80 CNG as the fallback. Litres typed from a receipt override it.
  • Needs the scanner left plugged in with auto-record on. Without drive data, the card simply doesn't appear.
