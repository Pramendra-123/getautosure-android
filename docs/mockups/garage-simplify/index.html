The full list, only if they want it. Each step says what the owner gets and what they need to do, in plain words ("Starting point saved", not "Baseline saved").

"Needs your attention"

  • Headline = what it means for the owner: ₹ a month, or the risk.
  • Second line = why, in plain words ("too much air or too little fuel", not "fuel mix running lean").
  • One action per line ("See why and what to do").
  • The costliest or most urgent first. "Coming up" stays short.

"Getting to know your car"

  • Moved out of the warnings card, so it isn't read as problems.
  • Renamed "Still learning your car", with a progress bar and only the next step.
  • Full list behind "What's next", in plain words.
  • Disappears once all steps are done (or after 60 days, as today).
