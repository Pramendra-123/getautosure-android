How it works
  • Same car, same price. The AI's answer is saved for a week, for that make, model, variant, year, fuel, km band (10,000 km), city and owners. Everyone checking that car sees the same price, and we pay for the AI once.
  • Search first, then price. Gemini searches current listings (Cars24, Spinny, CarDekho, CarWale, OLX…) and must explain each step and name the listings. An answer with no explanation, or an impossible range, is thrown away.
  • Our formula is always there. It runs alongside the AI, takes over when the AI fails, and is shown underneath the AI price for comparison.
  • The PDF matches the screen: "AI market check" or "Our estimate", with the steps.
  • Cars not in our price list (older or rarer models) now get a price from the AI alone. Before, they got none.
