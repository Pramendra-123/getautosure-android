Look around the car: a quicker, clearer step

This is the step before the price, where the buyer adds what the scan can't see: the body, tyres, glass and cabin. Today it's a long list of counters plus star ratings. The new version has two short screens: tap the car where you see damage, then answer a few quick questions, including an accident check. Each answer still takes a tentative repair cost off the price.

What changes
  • One button, "Body looks clean, skip", gets past the step in a single tap when there's nothing to note.
  • Damage is marked on a picture of the car instead of being counted in a list. The same costs per panel are used as today.
  • The star ratings go. Tyres, glass and lights, AC, and cabin get Good / OK / Needs work, and "Needs work" adds a cost, so every answer counts towards the price.
  • The accident check is new. Any "Yes" becomes a red flag in the seller check, next to cleared codes, and shows in the PDF.
  • The PDF shows the marked car diagram, so a buyer can show the seller exactly which panels need work.
