Where the test drive and the expert call fit

The aim is to replace the mechanic visit, but neither extra may slow down the main check. The core five steps always end in a verdict, a price and a report. The test drive and the expert call are add-ons the buyer can do at any point, or skip. Whatever they add updates the same report, price and negotiation sheet. Below are three ways to place them; I recommend option A.

A · "Make this check stronger", after the price Recommended

The five steps stay exactly as they are and always reach the verdict and price. Under the verdict, two cards say what's still unchecked: "How it drives" and "A second opinion". Each one is optional. When the buyer finishes the test drive, the verdict, price, negotiation and PDF update, and the card turns green. The negotiation sheet sits in the same screen, right under the price.

Why this worksThe buyer always gets an answer in about 5 minutes. The add-ons come at the moment the buyer is deciding: "I've seen the price, now let me drive it." The expert sees the full report before the call, so the call is short and useful.
Watch outSome buyers will stop at the price and never drive. The card says plainly what's still unchecked: "Gearbox and brakes: not checked yet."
