"There's also an engine code the scan found. Can you show me when it was last serviced?"
"Based on all of this, ₹10.15 lakh is what I can do."
If the seller says…
"Tyres are fine, they'll run another year."Show the photo. Tread under 2 mm isn't safe in the rain; ask for ₹8k off at least.
"Other buyers are coming tomorrow."That's fine. Your offer stands till tonight. Don't go over ₹11.35 L.
"The code is nothing, we'll clear it."Clearing hides it, it doesn't fix it. A week after you buy, the re-scan will show whether it's back.

What's real and what isn't

CheckHowHonest limit
Gearbox slipRPM against speed in each gear; a sudden RPM rise without speed is a slipWorks on most automatics and manuals. Some cars don't report the gear, so we infer it from the ratio.
MisfiresMode 6 misfire counters and RPM wobble under loadMode 6 support varies by car; RPM wobble works on all of them
OverheatingCoolant temperature in traffic with the AC onReliable
Lean or rich runningFuel trims at steady speedReliable; points to a cause but doesn't name the part
Battery and startingVoltage dip while crankingOnly when the engine starts with the dongle already in
Brakes, suspension, steering noiseThe buyer taps a buttonOBD can't hear. We only record what the buyer heard.
