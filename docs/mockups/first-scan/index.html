The first scan, on the Garage home

On day 1 there's no mileage trend and often nothing wrong, so a big "Needs your attention" card would be empty. Instead, the first scan shows a check-up card: what was checked and found, the papers from the RTO record (with dates even when nothing is due), and one question we can't answer ourselves. The big attention card only appears when something actually needs attention.

How the home decides what to show

  • First scan: "Your car's first check-up" (always, so day 1 is never empty).
  • Something needs action: "Needs your attention", problems first, with an orange border.
  • Scans after that, nothing wrong: "Since your last check-up", a short "what changed" card.
  • Coming up (papers, service) is a small separate list, never mixed into the warnings.

No duplication

  • Mileage is shown on the home only twice: once as news ("your real mileage") and later only if it drops ("costing you ≈ ₹900 more").
  • Everything else about fuel stays in the Fuel chip and Wallet.
  • Papers show dates even when not due, because on day 1 that's useful to know. After that, only when due within 30 days.
